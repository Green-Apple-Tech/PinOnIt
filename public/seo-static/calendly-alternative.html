What is PinOnIt?

PinOnIt is appointment scheduling software for a small business, with reminders built in. Customers book online. You remind them by text, WhatsApp, or voice, then send what they need to sign. They do not download an app or create a PinOnIt account.

Who is PinOnIt for?

Small service businesses and independent operators: contractors, venues, lawn and pool routes, trades, and one-person shops who work from a phone — not a contract desk.

How booking works

You share a booking link. The guest picks a time on their phone. Google, Outlook, and Apple busy times stay blocked. NeverMiss can then text a reminder; they reply 1 to cancel or 2 to reschedule.

FAQ

Why use PinOnIt instead of a basic scheduling tool?

A booking link gets the appointment on the calendar. PinOnIt also reminds them by SMS, WhatsApp, or voice, and can send a waiver or agreement by text for them to sign without an app or an account.

Does PinOnIt replace every Calendly feature?

No. PinOnIt covers the basic booking-and-reminders use case many small businesses actually run, plus quotes and Sign-by-Text. Stay on Calendly if you need its full meeting-platform feature set.

Can I import from Calendly?

Yes. You can import event types and keep an old Calendly link working while you switch.

Do my customers need an account?

No. They open a normal text and sign or book in the phone browser.
