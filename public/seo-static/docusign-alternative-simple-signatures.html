FAQ

Does PinOnIt replace DocuSign for everything?

No. Use DocuSign (or a lawyer) for complex routing, bulk send, and deals that need that stack. Use PinOnIt when the signature has to happen by text on a phone.

Is Sign-by-Text an electronic signature under ESIGN?

It is designed to meet federal ESIGN Act requirements for electronic signatures and captures an audit record. PinOnIt does not provide legal advice.

Can they sign a PDF I already have?

Yes. Upload a named PDF once (attorney-reviewed waiver, for example) and send it as many times as you need.
