FAQ

Do SMS reminders start automatically?

No. You turn reminders on per booking or event. STOP works for opt-out. Message rates may apply.

Can I remind myself, not just the client?

Yes. Personal reminders and on-my-way texts are in the same product.

Is this the same as marketing SMS?

No. These are appointment reminders the guest opted into. We do not sell a blast campaign tool.
