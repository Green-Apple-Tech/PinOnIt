LONG DESCRIPTION (use this)
PinOnIt is a text-first office for small businesses: quote a job, get a signature, book a time, and send reminders by SMS. $8.99/month after a 14-day trial. Guests pick a time on their phone. NeverMiss can text a reminder; they reply 1 to cancel or 2 to reschedule. Sign-by-Text is an SMS link, a one-time code, and a finger signature. It is not a notary and not multi-signer closing software. You get paid on your own Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal. PinOnIt never takes a cut. It covers the basic versions of those jobs in one product. It does not replace every feature of Calendly, DocuSign, or a full accounting suite.

AUDIENCE
Small service businesses and independent operators: contractors, venues, lawn and pool routes, trades, and one-person shops who work from a phone — not a contract desk.
